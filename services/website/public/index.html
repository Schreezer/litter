New · Open-ended exploration

Every lesson has a door.

Most courses are a straight line. In Learnfold, the end of every page asks where you want to go next, and the course grows in that direction.

  • Go deeperThe mechanism or detail this page only touched on.
  • Go sidewaysA neighboring or contrasting idea that sheds new light.
  • Zoom outThe bigger picture this fits into, and why it matters.
  • Or your own questionType it, or select a passage and ask Why? How? or for an example.

Choose the next page.

Pick a door and Learnfold writes that page for you, linked to the lesson it came from.

Keep reading while it writes.

New pages are written in the background. A small banner tells you when one is ready, so you never wait on a spinner.

Never lose the thread.

Side trails branch off the map with a “You are here” marker. Pin a page as a goal, see open threads, or tie them together in one page.

FAQ

A few answers first.

What is Learnfold, in one line? +

An iOS app that turns a question, link, or document into a course you can read, explore, check yourself on, and expand.

Do I need to bring my own material? +

No. A question is enough. Links and documents give the course something concrete to build from, but they are optional.

Does it build the whole course at once? +

No. You approve a short plan first. Then lessons are written section by section, as you need them, and explorations are written in the background while you keep reading.

Can I get back to the original lesson? +

Yes. Every explored page links back to where it came from, the course map shows each trail with a “You are here” marker, and Continue returns you to the route you were following.

Which AI writes my course? +

Your choice. Hosted works with no setup, within daily usage limits. You can also use Apple Intelligence on supported devices, Codex with your own API key, or Hermes on your own server.

Can the explanations be wrong? +

Yes. Learnfold uses AI to write explanations, and AI makes mistakes. Treat it as a tutor rather than a textbook, and check important facts against a reliable source.

When can I use it? +
