FAQ

A few answers first.

What is Learnfold, in one line? +

An iOS app that turns a question, link, or document into a short course you can read, question, and expand.

Do I need to bring my own material? +

No. A question is enough. Links and documents give the course something concrete to build from, but they are optional.

Does every question create a new page? +

No. Quick questions get quick answers in chat. When an answer deserves more room, Learnfold can improve the lesson or give the idea a linked page of its own. It makes that call on its own, and you can override it either way: ask for a page, or ask it to keep the answer in chat.

Can I get back to the original lesson? +

Yes. Every new page links to the lesson it came from, and the course map shows the whole structure. Continue reading wherever you stopped.

Can the explanations be wrong? +

Yes. Learnfold uses AI to write explanations, and AI makes mistakes. Treat it as a tutor rather than a textbook, and check important facts against a reliable source.
