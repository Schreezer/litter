Support

Learnfold is currently in internal-alpha development. Here is how to get help or share feedback.

Email

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email chirag@forgeme.xyz.

Course generation

If a course or lesson stops generating, keep the course rather than recreating it. Open its settings, confirm the selected learning agent is available, and retry from the course.

Connected learning agents

Learnfold can use an available Apple on-device model or a connected Hermes server. Connection status and removal controls are available in Course Settings.
