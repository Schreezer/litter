Some features require a paid auto-renewing subscription purchased in the App Store:

Apple's on-device and Private Cloud Compute agents are free. The price and billing period for each plan are shown in the app, in your local currency, before you subscribe.

  • Billing: payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Auto-renewal: a subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.

  • Refunds: payments and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. To request a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support. If Apple refunds or revokes a purchase, access to the paid features ends.
  • Fair use: Hosted has daily usage limits to keep the service available for everyone. A subscription is for your own use and can be used on a limited number of devices.
  • Price changes: if the price changes, Apple will notify you and, where required, ask for your consent before you are charged the new price.
